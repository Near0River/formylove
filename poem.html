A Poem For You 📜

Hi baby, here's the poem I wrote for you so you can read it whenever you want! I love you <3
Andrea the sweetest light
A radiant light with a heart of gold,
A story of kindness waiting to be told.
With curly hair and a gentle grace,
Andrea brings warmth to every place.
She gives her best in all she stays to do,
With a spirit that is steadfast, deep, and true.
Patient and wise, with a listening ear,
She calms the storm and eases every fear.
Sincere in her words, sensitive and bright,
She guides those around her toward the light.
Though she is fragile, her strength is her own,
The purest devotion that I have ever known.
She offers forgiveness with a selfless hand,
The loveliest heart in all of the land.
It’s easy to love her, so simple to see,
She is everything perfect a person can be.
I stumbled in silence, I lost my own way,
With words left unspoken that I should have said.
But she deserves the world and nothing less,
A life full of wonder and true happiness.
